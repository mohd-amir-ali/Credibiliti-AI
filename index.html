CONCEPT 01 / BASEL III CAPITAL TELEMETRY

How Basel III Works (`PD x LGD x EAD = ECL`)

Instead of guessing risk, Basel III measures PD (12-month default probability), LGD (loss severity after collateral recovery), and EAD (loan exposure) to compute exact Expected Credit Loss (ECL).

CONCEPT 02 / SHAP ATTRIBUTION WATERFALL

How SHAP Protects Fairness

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) breaks the credit model open to show the exact positive or negative basis-point contribution of each factor-based on RBI Fair Practices, ECOA, and GDPR Art. 22.

How Both Concepts Help the Client
